Valid Anagram, step by step

Two words are anagrams when they use exactly the same letters the same number of times. freq has one slot per letter. Every letter of s pushes its slot up by 1, every letter of t pushes it down by 1. If the letters match, everything cancels and all 26 slots end at 0.

Why s[i] - 'a': letters are stored as numbers ('a' = 97, 'b' = 98, …). Subtracting 'a' turns 'a'…'z' into 0…25, which is exactly the slot in freq.

  
Why check the lengths first: different lengths can never be anagrams. It also keeps t[i] safe inside the loop, because the loop runs over s.length().

  
Why one array instead of two: +1 for s and −1 for t means "same letters" shows up as "everything is 0". A slot above 0 means s has extra of that letter; below 0 means t has extra.
