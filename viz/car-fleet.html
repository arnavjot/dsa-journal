Car Fleet, step by step

Work out when each car would reach the target on its own: time = (target − position) / speed. Then look at the cars from the one closest to the target backwards. A car that would arrive sooner or at the same time as the fleet ahead catches up and joins it (it can't pass). A car that's slower starts a new fleet. The stack holds one arrival time per fleet.

Why start from the car closest to the target: a car can only be slowed by cars ahead of it. Going front to back, the fleet ahead is always already known.

  
Why compare times, not speeds: a fast car far behind may still arrive later. Arrival time combines both. If your time ≤ the fleet ahead's time, you'd reach it before (or at) the target, so you merge and arrive with it.

  
Why time > st.top() only (no pop): a car that joins takes the fleet's (slower) time, so nothing changes on the stack. Only a slower car adds a new fleet.
