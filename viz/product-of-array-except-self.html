Product of Array Except Self, step by step

The answer at i is (product of everything left of i) × (product of everything right of i). Pass 1 writes the left products into res. Pass 2 walks back from the end, carrying the right product in suffix, and multiplies it in. No division needed.

Why res[0] = 1 and suffix = 1: nothing is to the left of index 0 and nothing is to the right of the last index; 1 is "multiply by nothing".
Why no division: dividing the total by nums[i] breaks when nums[i] is 0. Try "With a zero".
Why O(1) extra space: res is the output (doesn't count), and the right side is one running variable instead of a second array.
