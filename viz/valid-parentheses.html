Valid Parentheses, step by step

Every opening bracket waits on a stack for its partner. A closing bracket must match the most recent unmatched opener, which is exactly the top of the stack. Match → pop it. No match, or nothing to match → invalid. At the end the stack must be empty.

Why a stack: brackets close in the reverse order they opened. The last one opened must be the first one closed: last in, first out.

  
Why counting isn't enough: "([)]" has one of each, but the ) arrives while [ is still open. Only the stack notices that.

  
The two other ways to fail: a closer with an empty stack (")"), and openers left over at the end ("((").
