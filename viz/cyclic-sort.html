Cyclic sort, step by step

Values are 1..n, so value v has a home: index v − 1. Keep swapping each value into its home. The duplicate finds its home already taken, so it gets stuck in a wrong spot. Then scan for it.

Why it's O(n): every swap puts one value in its final home, and a value at home never moves again. So at most n swaps. Every other step is i++, at most n times. Catch: it changes nums, which LC 287 doesn't allow. Say that in the interview, then give Floyd.
