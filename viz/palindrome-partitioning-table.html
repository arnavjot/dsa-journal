Palindrome Partitioning with a palindrome table

Same backtracking, but first fill pal[i][j] (is s[i..j] a palindrome?) bottom-up: ends equal and the inside is a palindrome. Then every check during backtracking is a single table lookup instead of a two-pointer scan.

Why fill from the bottom row: pal[i][j] needs pal[i + 1][j − 1], the row below.
Why j − i < 2: pieces of length 1 or 2 have no inside; equal ends is enough.
Cost: O(n²) for the table, then the same backtracking with O(1) checks.
