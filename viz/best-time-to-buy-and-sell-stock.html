Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock, step by step

Walk the days once. mini is the cheapest price seen so far, which is the best day to have bought if you sell today. Today's profit is prices[i] − mini (the green part of the bar above the dashed line). profit keeps the best of those.

Why the cheapest price so far: you must buy before you sell, so on day i you can only have bought on a day up to i. The best of those is simply the lowest one, and mini already holds it.

  
Why update mini before profit: if today is a new low, prices[i] − mini is 0 (buy and sell the same day), which never beats the best. So the order is safe, and it never sells before buying.

  
A new low doesn't wipe out the best: try "New low later". profit keeps the old best until a bigger one turns up.
