Valid Sudoku, step by step

Go through the filled cells once. Each digit must be new in its row, its column and its 3×3 box, so keep a set for each of the 9 rows, 9 columns and 9 boxes. A repeat in any of the three → invalid. (Only the rules are checked; the puzzle doesn't have to be solvable.)

Box index: (i / 3) * 3 + (j / 3) numbers the boxes 0–8 left to right, top to bottom: i / 3 picks the band of rows, j / 3 the band of columns.
Why sets: each check is "have I seen this digit here before?", O(1) with a set. 27 small sets, one pass over 81 cells.
