Evaluate Reverse Polish Notation, step by step

In RPN the operator comes after its two numbers, so there are no brackets to worry about. Read left to right: numbers wait on a stack; an operator takes the top two off, combines them, and pushes the result back. What's left at the end is the answer.

Why b is popped first: the right-hand number was pushed last, so it's on top. For "4 2 −" that gives a = 4, b = 2 and 4 − 2 = 2. Swap them and you'd get −2. Order matters for − and /.

  
Division: C++ int division cuts toward zero, so 7 / −3 = −2 (not −3). Try "Negative division".

  
Why a stack: an operator always uses the two most recent values that haven't been used yet: last in, first out.
