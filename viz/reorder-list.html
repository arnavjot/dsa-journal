Reorder List, step by step

Three steps you already know. 1. middleNode: fast/slow finds the middle. 2. reverseList(mid): reverse the second half. 3. Weave: take one from the front half (hf), one from the reversed back half (hs), and repeat. The nodes never move; only their next pointers change.

Why reverse the second half: the order wants the last node, then the second-to-last… Reversing lets you take them from the front, one by one.
Why save temp twice: each relink cuts a node off from the rest of its half, so the next node of that half is saved first.
The last line: when the loop stops with hf not NULL, hf is the new last node; hf->next = NULL ends the list (otherwise it could point back into it).
Cost: O(n) time, O(1) space.
