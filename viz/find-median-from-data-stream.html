Find Median from Data Stream, step by step

Split the numbers into a smaller half (maxi, a max-heap: its top is the biggest of the small ones) and a bigger half (mini, a min-heap: its top is the smallest of the big ones). Keep their sizes equal or off by one. Then the median is one of the two tops, or their average. Each add looks at which side is bigger and whether the new number is above the current median, moving one top across when needed to stay balanced.

Why two heaps: the median only depends on the middle one or two numbers, which are exactly the two tops. Everything else just needs to stay on the correct side.
Why move a top across: if adding to the already-bigger side would make it 2 larger, its top moves to the other side first, so sizes stay within 1.
Cost: addNum O(log n), findMedian O(1).
